# Why these shapes: sources

Research summary (October 2026) on how fields keep a decision-maker oriented on work they don't do themselves. Read this only if you need to justify or adapt the method.

| Idea | From | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom line up front | US Army writing standard (AR 25-50) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLUF_(communication) |
| Commander's intent (purpose, key tasks, end state) | US Army mission command | https://www.army.mil/article/215297/mission_command_requires_sharp_commanders_intent |
| Confirmation brief / backbrief (receiver explains it back) | Military; Bungay, *The Art of Action* | https://www.strategy-business.com/article/11312a |
| SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation | Hospital handoffs (IHI) | https://www.ihi.org/resources/tools/sbar-tool-situation-background-assessment-recommendation |
| I-PASS, incl. "synthesis by receiver" | Hospital shift handoffs; best outcome evidence of these practices | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9923540 |
| Threats first, interactive briefings | Aviation crew resource management | https://skybrary.aero/articles/effective-briefings |
| Separate facts from judgment, state uncertainty, show relevance | US intelligence analytic standards (ICD 203) | https://irp.fas.org/dni/icd/icd-203.pdf |
| Decision records | Architecture Decision Records (Nygard) | https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions |

Caveats: most of these are respected practice, not controlled studies (I-PASS is the exception). The AI-agent material is recent and thin. Model-stated confidence is not calibrated, which is why the skill prefers "checked by running" vs "believed from reading".
